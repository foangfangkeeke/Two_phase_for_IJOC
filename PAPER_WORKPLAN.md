# Paper Workplan

## Project Positioning

- Target journal: Transportation Research Part B.
- Paper type: transportation optimization methodology paper.
- Application: wireless charging facility deployment and adaptive battery configuration under medium-term service-plan uncertainty.
- Main methodological contribution: an exact lifted reformulation and a two-phase partial integer L-shaped method with monotonicity-strengthened cuts.

## Paper Skeleton

1. Introduction
2. Literature Review
3. Problem Description and Formulation
4. Reformulation and Solution Method
5. Case Study and Computational Results
6. Discussion
7. Conclusion
8. Appendices
