\section{Model Notation}
\label{app:model-details}

Duty, visit, and traversal indices refer to scenario-specific occurrences; quantities indexed by these events omit the scenario index.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}
\caption{Sets and parameters}
\label{tab:notation}
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.25\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.69\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Sets and indices}} \\
$\Omega,\omega$ & Service-plan scenarios and scenario index \\
$\mathcal S,\mathcal L$ & Stations and directed links, indexed by $s$ and $l$ \\
$\mathcal C,\mathcal L^{\mathrm D}$ & Stations with existing plug-in chargers and candidate DWC links, indexed by $c$ and $l$ \\
$\mathcal R,\mathcal K$ & Routes and battery-capacity options, indexed by $r$ and $k$ \\
$\mathcal M_{\omega,r}$ & Duties on route $r$ in scenario $\omega$, indexed by $m$; each duty corresponds to one bus \\
$\mathcal V,\mathcal A$ & Station-visit and link-traversal events across service plans \\
$\mathcal V_{\omega,r},\mathcal V^{\mathrm P}_{\omega,r}$ & Visits on route $r$ in scenario $\omega$ and the subset eligible for plug-in charging, indexed by $v$ \\
$\mathcal A_{\omega,r},\mathcal A^{\mathrm D}_{\omega,r}$ & Traversals on route $r$ in scenario $\omega$ and the subset on candidate DWC links, indexed by $a=(v,v')$ \\
$\mathcal T_\omega,\mathcal T_v$ & Relevant time slots in scenario $\omega$ and available charging slots for visit $v$, indexed by $\tau$ \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Parameters}} \\
$p_\omega$ & Probability of scenario $\omega$ \\
$c_l^{\mathrm D},c^{\mathrm{bat}}$ & DWC installation cost on link $l$ per representative day and battery capacity cost per kWh per representative day \\
$c^{\mathrm{day}},c^{\mathrm{night}}$ & Daytime and overnight electricity prices \\
$\beta_k$ & Battery capacity represented by option $k$ \\
$d_a,d_m^0,d_m^N$ & Energy consumption during traversal $a$, from the depot to the first visit of duty $m$, and from its last visit to the depot, respectively \\
$N_c$ & Number of existing plug-in chargers at station $c$ \\
$\bar q^{\mathrm P},\bar q_a^{\mathrm D}$ & Maximum grid-side energy per occupied plug-in slot and during DWC traversal $a$ \\
$\eta^{\mathrm P},\eta^{\mathrm D}$ & Plug-in and DWC charging efficiencies \\
$\underline\rho,\overline\rho$ & Minimum and maximum state-of-charge fractions \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
